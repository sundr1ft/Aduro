There's something uncomfortable about sharing unfinished work. But I think the
discomfort is the point.

Finished, polished work is easy to share. It's already been sanitized of the
wrong turns and half-baked ideas that got you there.

## The value of rough edges

When you share work in progress, you share the _process_. That's often more
useful to someone learning than the final artifact.

> The real voyage of discovery consists not in seeking new landscapes,
> but in having new eyes.

Something to keep in mind.
